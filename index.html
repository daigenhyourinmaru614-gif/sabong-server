Genetics Preview
🔵 Father  |  🩷 Mother — offspring blends both
ℹ️ How it works
F1 (First Cross): Named "F1 - Father x Mother". Chicks blend both parents but every trait varies, so none is a copy. An F1 is Crossbred; a bird only counts as a bloodline at 15/16 blood.
Inbreeding: Related parents expose hidden defects (crooked toes, timid, slow-witted, weak heart...) and lower fertility, but mild linebreeding inside one line can concentrate its best traits. Keep the best chicks, cull the flawed. A chick that hatches with a defect reveals both parents as carriers.
Station: High/Medium/Low station is genetically passed down. High station = taller bird = better fight odds.
Cross & Throwback Passives: every F1 of two different lines gets a unique cross passive (see the lookup in Bloodlines). From F2 on the cross passive is lost, but a crossbred chick may throw back to an ancestor line's passive (~34% at F2, less each generation).
Sani Bulik Offspring: Stats are 1/1000 of Sani's 1,000,000 power.
Mutation: ~5% chance of random stat spike.
